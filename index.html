How it plays

Trace words on a letter grid

Drag across touching tiles, sideways, up, down or corner to corner. Let go to play the word.

1

Pick a board

Boards come in three sizes, from a quick 4×4 to a roomy 6×6.

5×5

Find words before time runs out

Every real word you trace is taken and added to your score. When the clock hits zero, the round ends.

3

Go long

Longer words score more. One long find can beat a handful of short ones.
