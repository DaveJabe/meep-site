How it plays

Trace words on a letter grid

Drag across touching tiles, sideways, up, down or corner to corner. Let go to play the word.

1

Pick a board

Boards come in three sizes: the Small Board (4×4), the Big Board (5×5) and the Giant Board (6×6).

Find words before time runs out

Duel by turns

Play in Messages

In any iMessage chat, tap +, then Meep, to send a Duel by turns. Your friend taps the bubble to join, and you both play your turns right in the chat.

Questions

Good to know

When does it come out?+

January 2027, on the App Store. Until then you can play the beta: join it on TestFlight from your iPhone.

Is it free?+

Yes. Meep is free to play. Ads, and a way to pay to remove them, are planned for later. Nothing you can buy helps you score.

Which devices?+

iPhone, on iOS 18 or later. Duel by turns can also be played inside iMessage.

Is it coming to Android?+

Not at launch. Android and a version in the web browser are being looked at for after release.

Where can I play?+

In the United States and Canada.

How old do I need to be?+

13 or older.

Do I need an account?+

No sign-up. Tap New here? to start playing. Add Sign in with Apple in Settings if you want your account to move with you to a new phone.

Does it work offline?+

Playing on your own works with no connection. A round against other people that cannot be sent is saved and sent when you are back online.
