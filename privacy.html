Privacy Policy

Meep · Effective August 2, 2026

Meep does not track you, and shares nothing with anybody. There is no advertising, no analytics service, and no third-party SDK in the app. What it stores is what two people need in order to play the same board and compare scores honestly.

Your account

On first launch your device generates a random 32-byte secret and keeps it in the iOS Keychain. The server stores only its SHA-256, so a copy of the database is not a list of ways to sign in as its own rows. You are never asked to create an account and never shown a login screen.

Sign in with Apple

Optional, and only if you choose it. Meep receives the identifier Apple issues for this app and nothing else — it does not request your name or your email address. It exists so an account can survive a new phone.

Your handle

If you choose one, it is public and searchable by design: a handle is how someone finds you to send a board.

Rounds you play against other people

Meep stores the score and a log of the words and timings, which the server replays to check that a claimed score is one the board could actually produce. This is what makes a competitive result mean anything. Rounds you play on your own stay on your device.

Reports

If you report someone, Meep stores the reason and any note you write, so that a person can read it.

Network address

Your address is used briefly to apply rate limits and handle abuse. It is not used to build a profile and is not joined to anything else.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. It destroys every credential, session and device record, so nothing can sign in as you again, and clears your handle and display name.

One thing deliberately survives. Games you have already finished stay in your opponents’ history, without your name on them — a completed challenge is two people’s record, and the other player’s result is not ours to delete. Reports are kept for the same reason.

Children

Meep is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data: github.com/DaveJabe/Meep/issues.
