Playing someone else

  • New on the Boards screen sends a board to a friend, to a party of friends, or to someone new: a stranger who is also waiting, matched by rating. You both play the same letters under the same rules.
  • A friend is added with a friend code, from your contacts, or by handle. Nobody is your friend until they accept.
  • Neither of you sees a score until you have both finished.

If someone is a problem

Open their profile (tap their name or face anywhere it appears). Report offers five reasons (cheating, an offensive name, an offensive photo, harassment, something else) and a note for anything else, and ticks Block for you at the same time. Block hides you from each other: they cannot find you, invite you, or be matched with you, and a board between you that nobody has played yet ends. A board you had both already accepted keeps running to its result. Blocking is reversible under Settings → Blocked players.

Report sends us the details, and a person reads it. What can follow is a warning, a replacement handle, a suspension for a number of days, or the photo being removed. Reports are kept. The person you report is not told who reported them.

If you were warned, renamed or suspended and think it was a mistake, write to the address at the bottom of this page with your handle.

Keeping your account

Your account lives on your phone until you attach Sign in with Apple, which is what carries it to a new one: Settings → Account → Recovery. Without it, a lost or replaced phone loses the account and everything on it.

The subscription

Meep Premium is a monthly subscription bought through the App Store. Settings → Subscription shows where it stands, restores a purchase on a new phone, and opens Apple’s page to manage or cancel it. Deleting the app or your Meep account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. What goes and what stays is spelled out on the privacy page. If you cannot sign in, write to the address below.

Connection problems

Playing on your own works with no connection at all. If a competitive round cannot be sent, Meep saves it and delivers it when it can.
