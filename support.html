Support

Meep · Updated August 2, 2026

Drag through touching letters, find as many words as you can before the clock runs out. Longer words are worth more.

Playing someone else

  • Challenges → Challenge a friend gives you a code to share. They enter it under I have a code, and you both play the same letters under the same rules.
  • Challenge by handle sends a board to someone whose handle you know. Set your own under Settings → Your handle.
  • Neither of you sees a score until you have both finished.

If someone is a problem

Every challenge card has a menu. Block stops them sending you boards and stops your codes working for them. Report sends us the details, and a person reads it. Blocking is reversible under Settings → Blocked players.

Keeping your account

Your account lives on your phone until you sign in with Apple, which is what carries it to a new one: Settings → Keep your account.

Connection problems

Playing on your own works with no connection at all. If a competitive round cannot be sent, Meep saves it and delivers it when it can.

Anything else

Bugs, questions, and requests about your data: github.com/DaveJabe/Meep/issues.
